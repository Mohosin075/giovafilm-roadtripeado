Roadtripeado User Types and Access Levels
1. General Context
Roadtripeado will have an independent map for each country available on the platform.
Each map will mainly include two types of content:
Tourist Attractions- Waterfalls- Caves- Beaches- Rivers- Scenic viewpoints- Parks- Museums- Tourist attractions- Other points of interest
Businesses- Restaurants- Coffee shops- Accommodations- Rental businesses- Jet ski rentals- Tours- Transportation- Tourist experiences- Other tourism-related businesses
Businesses will be able to offer exclusive discounts to users who have purchased the
corresponding country's map.
Each map will be purchased through a one-time payment. Business listings will require
a separate monthly subscription.
User 1: Registered User Without a Purchased Map
Description
A user who has created an account and signed in but has not yet purchased any map.
This user does not necessarily have an active business plan, although they may
purchase one at any time without first buying a map.
Available Content
This user may:- Browse the platform.- View businesses available on the different maps.- View public information about restaurants, experiences, rentals, and other
businesses.- Purchase a map with a one-time payment.- Purchase a monthly business plan.
Locked Content
This user may not:- View tourist attraction information.- Access waterfalls, beaches, caves, rivers, viewpoints, and other premium tourist
locations.- Use exclusive business discounts.- Access other premium benefits included with a purchased map.
Premium features should remain visible but locked.
When the user taps a locked feature, the system should display a message similar to:
“This information and these benefits can be unlocked by purchasing your favorite
map.”
The message may include a button leading to the purchase page for that map.
Important Rule
Purchasing a map only unlocks the information and benefits for the purchased country.
For example:- Purchasing the Puerto Rico map unlocks premium access only for Puerto Rico.- Maps for the United States, Peru, Colombia, and other countries remain locked until
purchased individually.
User 2: User With One or More Purchased Maps
Description
A user who has made the one-time payment to purchase one or more country maps.
Each map purchase must be recorded individually by country.
Available Access
For every purchased map, this user may:- View all tourist attractions.- View waterfalls, beaches, caves, rivers, viewpoints, parks, and other attractions.- View all listed businesses.- Access complete information for attractions and businesses.- Use exclusive business discounts.- Access all premium benefits included in that map.
Restrictions- Premium access is limited to purchased maps only.- No automatic access to maps from other countries.- Cannot edit tourist attractions.- Cannot edit other people's businesses.- Cannot manage general system information.
Monthly Business Plan
This user may also purchase a monthly business plan.
Buying a map is not required to purchase a business plan. Maps and business
subscriptions are independent products.
User 3: User With a Monthly Business Plan
Description
Any user who purchases a monthly plan to add a business to the selected country's
map.
Purchasing the country's map is not required to register a business.
The purpose of the plan is to increase the business's visibility, attract new customers,
and allow it to offer an exclusive discount within Roadtripeado.
Allowed Business Types
Examples include:- Restaurants- Coffee shops- Accommodations- Vehicle rentals- Jet ski rentals- Tours- Experiences- Transportation- Tourism-related businesses
Business User Permissions
This user may:- Add one business to the selected map.- Complete the business information.- Edit only the business registered through their plan.- Add photos, descriptions, business hours, address, and contact details.- Configure the exclusive discount offered.- View business statistics.- View how many users visited or viewed the business profile.- View how many times the exclusive discount has been redeemed.
One Business Per Plan Rule
Each monthly plan allows only one business.
To add multiple businesses, a separate monthly plan is required for each business.
Restrictions
This user:- May edit only the registered business.- May not edit other businesses.- May not edit tourist attractions.- May not manage users.- May not modify maps.- May not manage general system information.- Does not receive premium map access by having a business plan.- Does not have access to the dashboard.
To access tourist attractions and use exclusive discounts, the corresponding map must
be purchased.
